# JUDAS // ECOSYSTEM CONTRACT

JUDAS is the narrative and immersive layer of the wider BELENTANI ecosystem.

## Position

```text
BELENTANI
   │
   ├── identity / artist / technology
   │
   └── JUDAS
        ├── narrative
        ├── music
        ├── visual language
        ├── interactive experience
        └── future/mobile/era surfaces
```

JUDAS should remain artistically autonomous while sharing system conventions with BELENTANI and OMEGA.

## Shared contract

Every JUDAS surface should document:

- `identity` — which era/experience it represents
- `media` — audio, visual, text or interactive assets
- `interaction` — what the audience can do
- `state` — prototype, live, archived or experimental
- `integration` — links to related BELENTANI/OMEGA systems

## No forced monolith

Separate repositories are intentional when they preserve a distinct experience, deployment target or creative era. The unification layer is documentation, metadata, design tokens and reusable interfaces—not destructive merging.

## Direction

**JUDAS is not a subpage of BELENTANI. It is a world inside the ecosystem.**
